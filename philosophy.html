DECISION GOVERNANCE

Our Five Decision Principles

Every commercial recommendation made by MOYKA is tested against five fundamental questions before reaching our clients:

01

Does This Create Long-Term Value?

Short-term commercial gains should never compromise long-term business sustainability, brand equity, or stakeholder trust.

02

Does This Reduce Commercial Risk?

Growth strategies must be ambitious, but never reckless. Every risk must be identified, quantified, and systematically mitigated.

03

Does This Strengthen Competitive Advantage?

Every strategic move should make the business more difficult to compete against and harder for rivals to replicate in Saudi Arabia.

04

Is This Commercially Practical?

Brilliant concepts have zero value if they cannot be executed effectively with available resources, capabilities, and market timelines.

05

Does This Support the Long-Term Vision?

Every tactical decision must move the organization one step closer to its 5-year strategic objectives in the Kingdom and wider GCC.

06

Decision Culture

We encourage executive teams to build cultures where decisions are data-informed, commercially grounded, customer-focused, and open to constructive challenge.
